## Project Title
Cyber Threat  Monitoring Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project shows cyber threats and it's impact on industry and country from 2015 - 2024. The dashboard provides total cyber threats,
total affected users, total financial loss, and etc-etc.

## Tools Used
--> MySQL 8.0.46
     - WHERE Clause
     - GROUP BY Clause
     - VIEWS
--> Microsoft Power BI 2.156.956.0

## Objectives
- To track country-wise cyber threats incidents and security trends.
- To measure and evaluate incident/case resolution time performance.
- To identify financial loss in each industry.
- To identify year wise total financial loss.
- To identify most vulnerability cause and defense strategy.
- To improve threat monitoring and incident management efficiency.
- To support faster and data-driven cybersecurity decision-making.

## Insights
- Zero-Day is the major security risk.
- Most of users are affected by DDos attack.
- IT and Banking industry are most targeted industry for financial attack.
- Weak Passwords is one of the reason for cyber threats.
- Antivirus is the most used defense strategy to prevent cyber threats.
- Most cyber threats was happened in UK.
- Most of cyber threats incidents was solved in 10 Hours.
- Unknown Sources was mainly Targeted IT industry.
